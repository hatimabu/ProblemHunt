## Account requests and operations

Signed-in owners can download their community JSON from their profile and submit or cancel a private account-deletion request. Trusted moderators can mark a request under review. These controls are implemented and locally verified; they require the pending privacy migration. Review does not perform erasure or promise a completion time. Before any operator removal, recheck that the request is still active and obtain approval for the exact data treatment. The download excludes avatar binaries/provider logs; support handles those separately.

Before public launch, set the repository variable `SUPPORT_CONTACT` to a monitored `mailto:` address or HTTPS contact page; the build exposes this as `VITE_SUPPORT_CONTACT`. `community-release-config.mjs` refuses a deployment build without a valid contact or with notifications enabled. This checks syntax, not actual mailbox ownership or monitoring. The owner must supply a working support/privacy contact and identify who checks reports and requests. Do not invent an email address or claim someone monitors it. Until configured, the old pilot invitation-channel copy is not adequate for open registration.

For an export, verify ownership through the signed-in account or its verified email; never accept a supplied UUID alone. Use `supabase/operations/account-export.sql` in a read-only session with the verified UUID. It exports authored community content (including private records), profile, preferences, own votes/reports, reputation/acceptance history, rate-limit records, inbox and recipient delivery metadata. It excludes Auth credentials, private moderation notes and other users' authored content. Review the result, separately retrieve the account's current avatar through an authorized private Storage channel, and assess historical account/provider records not covered by the community export. Deliver only via a verified private channel. Keep working files encrypted/access-controlled and outside Git; remove temporary copies after confirmed delivery according to the owner's approved retention policy.
