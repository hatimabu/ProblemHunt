# Pilot tester checklist

Use only fictional or approved anonymized cases. Never paste credentials, customer names, internal addresses or proprietary logs. Example acceptance demonstrates the workflow; it is not a real verified fix.

1. Anonymous visitor: open both domain pages; search a symptom, product and tag; filter Open/Solved; refresh a direct problem and solution link. A private draft URL must show unavailable. Distinguish a proposal, author acceptance and upvotes.
2. Author A: create a private draft, edit it, refresh, then publish. Ask B to propose a solution. Record tests and observations, mark Testing, confirm a solution, and refresh. Withdraw acceptance with a reason, check preserved history, retest and reaccept. Close a separate unresolved case.
3. Contributor B: verify A's draft is inaccessible. Propose a solution and clarification; verify self-voting is unavailable and acceptance/reversal cannot be performed for A. A can add/remove an upvote; observe counts. Reputation is category-specific and examples earn none.
4. Reporter: report public content. Another ordinary account cannot read the report, and even the reporter cannot read moderator notes.
5. Dedicated moderator: review the report, add private notes, hide and restore the fictional discussion. Anonymous/contributor reads and search must stop while hidden. Moderator access to A's draft remains denied.
6. Phone/keyboard: complete navigation and forms at 390px, check focus visibility and skip link, and refresh every direct route. Report confusing controls or clipped content.
7. Privacy: local pilot counts start off; opt in, submit a search and inspect only numeric counts. Opt out and verify deletion. Do not send screenshots containing account credentials. Error monitoring must not capture content, query text or session replay.

Record: browser/device, route, role, expected vs actual outcome and a reproducible sequence. Keep reports free of secrets. Contact the pilot organizer through the invitation channel for account export/deletion or urgent accidental disclosures. No invitations are sent by these scripts.
